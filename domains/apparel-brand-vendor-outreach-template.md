# Apparel Brand — Vendor Outreach Template

Built 2026-08-05, tied to `domains/apparel-brand-system.md`'s vendor
selection criteria and the rehearsed first action from
`agents/success-generator/sessions/2026-08-05-session-3.md`: contact 2–3
candidate manufacturers for samples.

## How to use this

Send this to 2–3 candidate small-batch bulk manufacturers (MOQ in the
50–150 units/design range) before committing to any one of them. Fill in
the bracketed sections with your real details. Track responses in the log
table at the bottom so the comparison is visible, not just remembered.

---

## Message template

> Subject: Small-batch production inquiry — [X] units, [garment type]
>
> Hi [Manufacturer name / contact name],
>
> I'm launching an apparel brand and looking for a small-batch production
> partner for an initial run. Details:
>
> - **Garment type:** [e.g. heavyweight cotton tee, hoodie]
> - **Quantity:** [50–150] units for the first design, with more designs
>   and larger runs planned if this first run goes well
> - **Print/decoration method:** [e.g. screen print, DTG, embroidery —
>   fill in once decided]
> - **Timeline:** looking to place a first order within [X weeks]
>
> Before committing, I'd like to:
> 1. Get your minimum order quantity and per-unit pricing at that
>    quantity.
> 2. Get a paid sample made from my actual design, not a stock/blank
>    sample.
> 3. Understand your typical production lead time from order placed to
>    ready-to-ship.
> 4. If possible, a reference or review history from a comparable small
>    brand you've worked with.
>
> Could you send over pricing and sample-order details? Happy to share
> the design file once we're talking specifics.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]
> [Brand name / contact info]

---

## Selection criteria (from domains/apparel-brand-system.md — don't skip)

Evaluate every response against these, not just price:
- MOQ fits the 50–150 unit small-batch range
- Sample turnaround time
- Quoted production lead time
- Quality of the **paid sample** (not just photos/portfolio)
- At least one verifiable reference or review history

## Next step once samples are in hand

Compare the 2–3 samples against the criteria above, pick the primary
vendor, and identify the runner-up as the documented backup (per
`domains/apparel-brand-system.md`'s vendor management process) — not used
yet, just identified, so a primary-vendor failure isn't a from-scratch
search.
