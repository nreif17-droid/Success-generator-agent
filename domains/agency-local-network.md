# Agency — Sedona/Verde Valley Local Industry Network

Built 2026-08-05, from research Nolan supplied on the local STR/property
management ecosystem. Purpose: personal industry knowledge, local
presence/credibility, and — for at least one of these — a real
client-acquisition channel, not just networking for its own sake.

## Organizations, cadence, location, how to get in

### AZREIA (Arizona Real Estate Investors Association) — Sedona Chapter
- **Focus:** connects independent STR investors, corporate property
  managers, and trade vendors. Runs a formal "Preferred Vendors Program"
  bridging real estate operators and vetted maintenance/emergency trades
  — worth understanding how that program works, possibly worth getting
  listed in eventually.
- **Core event:** Sedona Real Estate Networking Night, ~monthly.
- **Next known instance:** Thursday, October 23, 2026, 6:00–8:00 PM.
- **Venue:** Vino Di Sedona, 2575 Arizona 89A, Sedona, AZ 86336.
- **How to get in:** check azreia.org for guest/visitor policy — real
  estate investor associations typically allow paid guest attendance at
  networking events before requiring full membership.
- **URL:** https://azreia.org/

### Greater Sedona Chamber of Commerce & Tourism Bureau
- **Focus:** B2B networking, local policy tracking, small business
  workshops. Promotes local trade integration via a "Home Services"
  directory/marketing framework.
- **Core events:**
  - Monthly Business Mixers — next known instance **August 19, 2026**,
    Mountain Modern Sedona. High-density regional-operator networking.
  - Brand Camp / Marketing Workshops — technical workshops for
    independent operators and local service firms.
- **How to get in:** check sedonachamber.com for RSVP/guest policy —
  Chamber mixers commonly allow at least one prospective-member guest
  visit. Chamber membership itself may be worth it for local credibility
  beyond just event access.
- **URL:** https://sedonachamber.com/

### Sedona Lodging Council
- **Focus:** non-profit trade association for traditional hospitality,
  resorts, and large-scale regional lodging operators. Note: worth going
  in aware that traditional lodging/resort interests and STR growth
  sometimes have real friction in tourist markets — approach with that
  context, not naively.
- **Meetings:** 2nd Tuesday of each month, 8:00–10:00 AM, Sedona Public
  Library. **On recess July and August** — next meeting is likely
  **Tuesday, September 8, 2026** (2nd Tuesday of September).
- **How to get in:** check sedonalodgingcouncil.com for visitor policy.
- **URL:** https://sedonalodgingcouncil.com/

### Arizona Multihousing Association (AMA)
- **Focus:** hosts **"Reverse Trade Shows"** — PMCs explicitly interview
  and onboard external independent maintenance/emergency-response
  contractors. **This is the one with direct client-acquisition
  potential, not just educational value** — worth prioritizing once
  there's something concrete to show (ties to the agency's actual
  current priority, not just personal knowledge-building).
- **Cadence:** not fixed/monthly — check azmultihousing.org for
  scheduling.
- **URL:** https://www.azmultihousing.org/

### Arizona Association of REALTORS®
- **Focus:** holds Property Management Workshops on legal frameworks for
  independent contractor use and vendor compliance — directly relevant
  to understanding the compliance/liability side of this niche.
- **Cadence:** occasional — check aaronline.com for scheduling.
- **URL:** https://www.aaronline.com/

### Verde Valley Business Referrals Club (VVBRC)
- **Focus:** weekly structured lead-exchange group, strict
  one-business-per-profession rule (BNI-style).
- **Meetings:** Wednesday mornings, Cottonwood, AZ.
- **How to get in:** most groups in this format allow visiting before
  joining — check whether an "STR automation" or comparable category is
  already filled (the one-per-profession rule means it might not be
  open). Most accessible/frequent entry point on this list given the
  weekly cadence.

## What this confirms/adds for the agency itself

- **PMS platforms in this specific region:** Guesty, Hostaway, Track, and
  Buildium — often auto-dispatching work orders to external vendor
  portals. Cross-referenced into `domains/agency.md`'s open PMS-scoping
  item.
- **Vendor-dispatch tools, possibly more relevant than the PMS itself:**
  Breezeway and Turno — worth understanding whether the agency's service
  competes with, complements, or should integrate alongside these,
  not just the PMS.
- **Operational triggers driving PMC vendor demand** — useful context
  for the research call flow (`domains/agency-research-call-flow.md`):
  emergency dispatching (HVAC, plumbing, lockouts, storm damage),
  seasonal readiness cycles (pre-monsoon, pre-winter), and compliance
  requirements (general liability insurance, workers' comp, Arizona ROC
  licensing) — these are the actual pain points to listen for in calls,
  not abstract ones.
