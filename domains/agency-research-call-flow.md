# Agency — Research Call Flow

Built 2026-08-05, for the current market-research phase of outreach (not
a sales call — see `domains/agency.md`). Designed to extract the two
qualification data points, resolve the open PMS-platform question, and
capture problem language for the weekly review, in one short call.

## Who to ask for

**Primary target: Operations Manager / Director of Operations** (title
varies by firm size — sometimes "VP of Operations"). They know the
actual day-to-day manual workflow and the real labor cost behind it, and
they're far more reachable cold than an owner/principal.

**For smaller firms:** the owner often *is* the operations person —
treat title flexibly, target whoever owns the actual workflow.

**Fallback:** a senior property manager who isn't the final
decision-maker is still a valuable conversation right now — this phase
is about gathering signal, not closing. Don't skip a call just because
the person on the line can't say yes to anything.

## The call flow

**Keep it to 3-5 minutes. No pitch. The goal is information, and being
genuinely research-framed (not sales-framed) usually gets more honest
answers, not less.**

### 1. Opening — disarm and frame as research (10-15 sec)

> "Hi, is this [Name]? My name's [Name] — I'm doing some research into
> how property management companies handle [specific task, e.g. guest
> communication / turnover scheduling / booking coordination]. I'm not
> selling anything today, just trying to understand how teams like yours
> handle this right now. Got 3-4 minutes?"

If no: "No problem — is there a better time, or someone else on your
team who'd know this side of things best?" (Don't push past a real no —
log it and move to the next touch.)

### 2. Warm-up / role confirmation

> "Before I dive in — what's your role there, and roughly how many
> properties does your team manage?"

Gives you firm size and confirms you're talking to someone with real
visibility into the process.

### 3. Core discovery — qualification criterion 1 (manual or automated?)

> "Walk me through how [the specific task] gets handled day to day right
> now — is that something a person on your team is doing manually, or do
> you already have software handling most of it?"

This is the single most important question — it directly answers
qualification criterion 1.

### 4. Core discovery — qualification criterion 2 (real labor cost?)

> "About how many people, or how much time, is tied up in that process
> week to week?"
>
> "If that got automated or cut down to a fraction of the time, would
> that actually move the needle for your team — or is it not a big
> enough cost to matter?"

The second question matters as much as the first — a manual process that
nobody cares about the cost of isn't qualified, per the criteria on file.

### 5. Current tools / PMS platform (resolves the open item in `domains/agency.md`)

> "What system are you using to manage the properties day to day —
> AppFolio, Buildium, Guesty, something else?"

Every answer here is useful data even beyond this specific prospect —
it's building the picture of which PMS to scope the first integration
against.

### 6. Pain point capture (open-ended — feeds the Friday review's
   "recurring problem language" tracking)

> "What's the most frustrating part of that whole process for your team
> right now?"

Let them talk. Don't interrupt to solve it — just capture the exact
language they use.

### 7. Close — soft, permission-based, no pitch

> "This is really helpful. I'm working on something that might address
> exactly this — still early, but would it be alright if I followed up
> once I have something concrete to show you?"

Gets permission for a real sales conversation later without turning this
call into one now.

## Logging each call

Right after the call, while it's fresh, log against the qualification
criteria in `domains/agency.md`:
- Manual process? Yes/No
- Real labor cost tied to it? Yes/No → **Qualified** if both are yes
- PMS platform used
- Exact pain-point language (verbatim, for the Friday review)
