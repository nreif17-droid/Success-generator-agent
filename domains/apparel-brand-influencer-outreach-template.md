# Apparel Brand — Micro-Influencer Outreach Template

Built 2026-08-05, tied to `domains/apparel-brand-system.md` §7
(Audience building). This is the product-for-post seeding arm of the
audience-first strategy in `domains/apparel-brand-financial-model.md` —
costs product, not ad spend.

## How to use this

Send to candidates in the ~5,000–50,000 follower range in the
fitness/gym niche. Track responses in the log below so seeding decisions
are visible, not just remembered. **Unit allocation still open** — see
`domains/apparel-brand-system.md` §7: 10 units total out of the 100-unit
Phase 1 run, or 10 per garment (20 total)? Confirm before sending real
offers, since it changes how many creators can actually be seeded.

---

## Message template

> Subject: Partnership — free [garment] in exchange for a post
>
> Hey [Name/handle],
>
> I'm launching [brand name], a new fitness apparel brand — starting
> with a [hoodie / pump cover]. I've been following your content and
> think it's a genuine fit for what we're building.
>
> I'd love to send you a free [garment] (your size, on us) in exchange
> for one post wearing it — a workout clip, a fit check, whatever feels
> natural for your page. No script, just wearing it and tagging
> [@handle].
>
> If you're open to it, send me your size and shipping info and I'll get
> it out to you. Happy to answer anything about the brand first if
> useful.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]
> [Brand name / contact info]

---

## What to evaluate before sending

- Follower range fits ~5,000–50,000 (working definition, not a hard
  rule — genuine niche fit matters more than hitting an exact number)
- Actual engagement on recent posts (a big follower count with dead
  comments isn't worth a free unit)
- Content style/quality — does their existing content look like
  something the brand would want associated with it

## Note on the seeding decision

Whatever the final unit allocation is, keep track of it against sellable
inventory in `domains/apparel-brand-financial-model.md` — seeded units
are a marketing cost paid in product, not sellable stock, and should be
subtracted from the 100-unit Phase 1 count when projecting revenue.
