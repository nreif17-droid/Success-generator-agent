# Sales Job — Domain Status

Last updated: 2026-08-05 (Success Generator session — see
`agents/success-generator/sessions/2026-08-05-session-2.md`)

## Status
Starts Monday, 2026-08-10. Commission-based sales role.

- Structure: ~1 hour drive each way, on-site by 8am. Appointments through
  the day (example day: 10am, 2pm, 6pm — potentially more).
- Onboarding: first 2 weeks training in the office. Then paired with
  someone for another 1–2 weeks. After that, on his own — driving to
  jobs/appointments and closing on behalf of the business, on commission.
- Long days once appointments start, given the drive plus a full
  appointment schedule.

## Active goal (set 2026-08-05)
Minimum **18% close rate** (stretch/objective: 20%) at 15–18 appointments
a week, within **90 days of the 2026-08-10 start — by 2026-11-08**.

## Friction / blocker
Biggest concern named: the time commitment the job will take (long
days — drive + full appointment schedule) and how that squeezes capacity
for the other ventures, particularly the agency (top priority per the
priority order in `vision.md`). Also identified as a live risk: close
quality dropping later in the day (e.g. a 6pm appointment) as fatigue
builds across a long day — the system below tracks appointment time slot
specifically to catch this if it's real.

## System (set 2026-08-05)
- **First 2 weeks (office training, no real appointments yet):** daily
  minimum — one line logged on the single most useful thing learned that
  day, so the training period produces something trackable rather than a
  blank two weeks.
- **Paired weeks onward (real appointments):** daily minimum — log every
  appointment same day: outcome (closed/not) and time slot. Stretch: same
  plus one line on what he'd do differently.
- **Weekly review (Sunday night or first thing Monday, ahead of the
  week's appointments):** tally appointments run, closes, close rate for
  the week against the 18% minimum / 20% stretch; note which time slots
  underperformed (fatigue check); decide one adjustment for the coming
  week.
- **Tracking:** `domains/sales-job-tracking.md`, via `agents/habit-tracker.md`.
- **Accountability:** no mastermind currently; the weekly review itself is
  the accountability point for now — same structure as the agency.
