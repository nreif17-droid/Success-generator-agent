# Cross-Venture Daily/Weekly Checklist

Built 2026-08-05, per direct request for something to keep day-to-day
work organized and correctly prioritized across all active ventures. This
is the tactical layer — `master-plan.md` is the 12-month strategic view,
this is "what do I actually do today." **Living document — update the
"Right now" section whenever a venture's status changes** (sales job
starting, apparel entering execution, grid moving from research to
building).

## Priority order (restated plainly, from `master-plan.md`)

1. **Agency** — top priority, non-negotiable. It's not just "most
   important" in the abstract — it's the funding source for apparel and
   the proof-of-concept for grid. Under-investing here delays two other
   ventures, not just this one.
2. **Sales job** — parallel commitment (starts 2026-08-10), independent
   income, not currently linked to funding any other venture.
3. **Apparel** — secondary and opportunistic. Real work, but scheduled
   *after* agency and sales-job obligations are met, not instead of them.
   Named risk from the apparel session: this is the venture most likely
   to be used as a comfortable escape from the harder work above it —
   check yourself against that, not just the task list.
4. **Grid** — research/outreach only right now, long horizon (18-30mo).
   Minimal time draw by design.
5. **Coaching** — on hold. Not on this checklist until reactivated.

## Daily checklist

**Do these in order — don't let #3 happen before #1 and #2 are done.**

- [ ] **Agency:** outreach touches (3 minimum / 6 stretch, weekdays) —
      logged in Nolan's own tracking sheet
- [ ] **Sales job** *(once started 8/10):* same-day log of appointments
      (outcome + time slot) or, during training weeks, one line on the
      day's key learning
- [ ] **Apparel** *(after 1 & 2 are done):* one piece of content posted
      (audience-building daily minimum) + whatever the active task is
      this week (vendor outreach, mockup review, influencer outreach)
- [ ] If the avoidance pull shows up anywhere above: name it, return to
      the breath, before deciding whether to skip the task

## Weekly checklist

Pick one weekly slot (Friday, per the agency's existing cadence, or
Sunday night/Monday morning per the sales job's — don't run two separate
weekly reviews if one slot can cover both).

- [ ] **Agency Friday review:** tally touches/replies/calls, log
      recurring objection/problem language, decide one adjustment
- [ ] **Sales job weekly review** *(once started):* appointments/closes/
      close rate vs. 18% min / 20% stretch, fatigue-by-time-slot check,
      one adjustment
- [ ] **Apparel weekly review:** follower count, content posted vs.
      daily minimum, waitlist signups, vendor/influencer outreach status
- [ ] **Refresh `sessions/CURRENT-STATE.md`** if any new session got
      logged this week (`agents/session-carry-forward.md`)
- [ ] **Sanity-check `master-plan.md`'s capacity assumptions** against
      what actually happened this week, especially real sales-job hours
      once that's known — this is the plan's single biggest open unknown
