# Session — 2026-08-05 (third session this day — apparel brand)

## Goal brought to the session
Build the A-to-Z operating system for the apparel brand: market
research, profitability, scaling, product/inventory, shipment/orders,
vendor management — toward maximum automation once running.

## 1. Clarify — definiteness of purpose
- Fulfillment model decided: small-batch bulk (low-MOQ manufacturer,
  ~50–150 units/design), not print-on-demand/dropship — chosen for
  product/culture control, capital exposure bounded relative to full
  bulk.
- Sales channel: not finalized (Shopify tentative; an AI store-builder
  tool also being considered, name TBD).
- Specific target, split into two tracks after a timeline reality-check
  (see below):
  - Playbook/system itself: a few weeks from 2026-08-05.
  - Store live selling real bulk inventory: revised to ~Q1 2027
    (originally ~2026-11-05).
- Deadline: as above.
- How it will be measured: playbook existence + completeness
  (`domains/apparel-brand-system.md`) for track 1; real sales/inventory
  live for track 2.

## 2. Diagnose the gap
- Identity/habit/skill shift required: build the operating system now,
  decoupled from capital/product timing, so it's ready to execute
  instantly once agency capital and finished product exist — rather than
  inventing the process under pressure later.
- What's currently in the way: no vendor relationship, no fulfillment
  process, no profitability model, designs still in progress (Midjourney,
  mockups not started), zero capital committed.
- **Timeline check surfaced a real problem:** apparel's funding model
  ("agency capital rolls into apparel") didn't match the dates — the
  agency's own committed capital target (3 clients, ~$7,000 MRR) is
  2027-01-31, three months after apparel's original November live-date.
  Store-live target revised to ~Q1 2027 as a result; the playbook itself
  was split out as a separate, sooner, capital-independent target.

## 3. Build the system
Full A-to-Z system built in `domains/apparel-brand-system.md`: market
research (ongoing cadence + pre-bulk-order validation), vendor/supplier
management (selection criteria, sourcing process, backup vendor,
reorder trigger), product & inventory (SKU structure, tracking, QC),
order fulfillment (process, returns policy, shipping — several pieces
explicitly flagged as "decide once real data exists," not invented from
zero), profitability tracking (per-unit economics framework, monthly
review cadence, break-even framing — no numbers promised, framework
only), and a scaling/automation roadmap (order-volume, inventory-volume,
and marketing-automation triggers, all flagged as placeholders pending
real operating data).

- Daily/weekly practice: not yet defined at the habit level (this
  session built the process architecture, not a tracked daily/weekly
  habit) — revisit once execution actually starts.
- Tracking method: not yet built — revisit alongside `agents/habit-
  tracker.md` once there's a real system to track against (same pattern
  as agency and sales job).
- Mastermind/accountability structure: none — same open gap as the other
  two built-out ventures, per `sessions/CURRENT-STATE.md`.

Also covered as informational (not legal/financial advice), per direct
questions asked mid-session:
- LLC: apparel likely needs its own, separate from the agency's, given
  different risk profile (physical product liability) and to keep
  profitability tracking clean per venture.
- Business loan: a new pre-revenue LLC won't qualify for traditional
  bank/SBA financing on revenue history it doesn't have; realistic early
  options (personal loan, business credit card, SBA microloan,
  purchase-order financing) almost all require a personal guarantee —
  the LLC doesn't shield that specific debt. Framed as a real financial
  decision, not a shortcut around the agency-capital timeline.
