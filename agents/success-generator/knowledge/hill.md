# Napoleon Hill — *Think and Grow Rich* / *The Law of Success*

Status: **Core canon.** The definitional text of this lineage — most of what
comes after Hill is either a compression of his ideas or a re-packaging of
them. Treat as a practice system to mine for technique, not gospel.

## Core mechanisms

1. **Definiteness of purpose** — a specific goal, with a specific number and
   a specific deadline, converted into a written statement and a plan. Hill's
   single most-repeated claim, and the one with the strongest real backing
   (see "What's actually defensible" below).
2. **The mastermind principle** — two or more minds working in harmony toward
   a definite purpose produce a third, greater intelligence than either alone.
   Practically: a small, aligned, high-performing accountability group,
   meeting on a cadence, with real stakes.
3. **Persistence** — most failure in Hill's case studies is failure to
   persist past a temporary defeat. He treats persistence as a trainable
   habit (built through purpose + desire + self-reliance + organized
   planning), not a fixed trait.
4. **Autosuggestion** — repeated, emotionalized self-instruction (written
   goal statement, read aloud twice daily) as a way of directing the
   subconscious mind toward the goal. Modern translation: this is priming and
   rehearsal, not a supernatural channel — see the visualization note below.
5. **Organized planning** — a definite plan, put into action immediately,
   backed by a mastermind group if the individual lacks the necessary
   experience/knowledge alone.
6. **The "burning desire" chapter's real content** — despite the mystical
   framing, the actual instructions are: write the goal down, set a date, set
   what you'll give in exchange, read it aloud twice a day. This is closer to
   a proto-SMART-goal exercise than it looks.

## What's actually defensible (the mechanism layer)

- Definiteness of purpose maps cleanly onto Locke & Latham's goal-setting
  theory: specific, difficult goals outperform vague "do your best" goals
  across decades of replicated research.
- The mastermind principle maps onto real findings on accountability
  structures and peer-group effects on goal completion.
- Autosuggestion/repeated goal review functions as a rehearsal and
  attention-priming mechanism (see `modern-claims.md` for where this
  literature overstates itself vs. where it's supported).

## What to flag if surfaced at face value

- Hill's claim that thought has direct "vibrational" power to attract
  outcomes is not established; use it only as historical framing, never as a
  causal mechanism the agent endorses.
- The Andrew Carnegie/"500 successful men" research frame in *Think and Grow
  Rich* is largely undocumented — treat the case studies as illustrative
  narrative, not verified data.
